The studio

Ghouse Records started as a booth in Kiwatule built for artists who needed somewhere honest to record — no gatekeeping, no rush. Five years on it's a full studio, a small stable of signed acts, and a label that puts out what gets made here.

Most days, the room is booked for tracking, mixing and mastering. Once a month, on a Saturday, the gear gets cleared and the same floor turns into a silent disco — headsets on, one night, Kiwatule.
